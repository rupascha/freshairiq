# FreshAirIQ 0.26.5.3

Release on top of 0.26.5.2 with community and owner feedback: exhaust fans with neighbouring-room reference air, open connections between rooms, fewer and consistent messages (action vs. status, reminders, no floods), the room's temperature goal keeps a cooling window open, shared fans, removable sensors and settings that apply without a restart. Golden master: recommendations unchanged except one intended case – a bathroom at 88 % surface humidity closed two minutes earlier now waits instead of being asked to air again (GitHub #15); otherwise only new room fields. Recommendations also change for rooms with a temperature goal that are still above their comfort target while airing. German and English verified.

## Room setup (Devices & services)
- Exhaust fan supply air (community request): an exhaust fan blows the room air outside, the replacement air flows in from the neighbouring room (door gap, transfer grille) – not from outside. Each room now offers the optional pair "Exhaust fan supply air: temperature / humidity (neighbouring room)" in the sensor section. FreshAirIQ uses this air as reference while the fan airs the room: the fan runs and no window is open, or the room has no window contact at all (then the fan is its only way to air). An open window keeps using its own outdoor or opening-specific reference. Both sensors must be chosen together; if one is unavailable, FreshAirIQ does not silently fall back to outdoor air. Without the pair everything works as before.

## Community feedback (HarryP)
- Mould node flood: the `freshairiq_mould_risk` event carried the room action as its sentence ("Büro: Bitte Lüfter ausschalten."). It now speaks about the mould risk ("Büro: Schimmelrisiko hoch · Oberflächenfeuchte 82 %."); the action sentence stays available as `action_message`. A falling level is reported once it held 10 minutes, so a surface humidity hovering around a boundary no longer fires every 30 seconds.
- The whole-home event no longer fires every minute while its countdown runs ("noch ca. 19 min", "18 min", …). In a simulated day it fired 60 times an hour before, now at most twice.
- Action and status messages are separated: every event and notification carries `category` (action / status / warning). New setting "Send status messages" (on by default) and "Reminder of an open action" (5–60 min, off by default).
- No contradicting messages: room messages follow the recommendation aligned with the whole home – no "please air" while the house waits, no "please close" while the whole-home airing keeps the room open.
- Temperature goal is respected: a room with a temperature goal (thermostat, manual or fallback target) keeps airing while it is above its comfort target and the outdoor air is clearly cooler and brings in no moisture – also in the comfort profile and beyond the maximum airing time. Before, cooling only counted from 24 °C in the summer profile, so the bedroom aimed at 19 °C was told to close.
- Open connections: rooms connected without a door or contact (hallway without a door, serving hatch) can be set under Home & rooms → Open connections. Connected rooms count as aired together, a room is not asked to close while the connected room still airs, and passive airing of the connected room is estimated.
- Shared exhaust fans: a fan used by several rooms stays on while any room still needs it; "switch off the fan" is only said once no room needs it any more.

## Fixes
- Ventilation log per day and as CSV (user feedback): the log in Details → Ventilation log gets the option **Summarise per day**. The PDF then shows one row per day for the whole home – number of airings, minutes in total and per airing, moisture removed (with how many airings were measured) and the rooms – plus totals and averages for the period (days with airing, minutes per day and per airing, moisture per day). Next to the PDF button, **As CSV** exports the same data for spreadsheets, per airing or per day; the daily CSV lists days without airing too, so charts have no gaps. German uses semicolons and decimal commas, English commas and decimal points. An airing counts on the day it started; moisture only comes from measured airings.
- Less recorder load, nothing lost (community): FreshAirIQ wrote about 24,000 recorder rows a day in a two-room home, many without anything having changed. Entity attributes kept out of the database still write a row whenever they change, and several values changed with every calculation. Measured with a real Home Assistant recorder over the same simulated three hours: 3,000 → about 1,700 rows (−43 %); the status sensor −94 %, a quiet room's action sensor −60 %, the overnight forecast −92 %, the forecast temperature change −97 %. How: the dashboard (card and panel) receives its data over a live channel (`freshairiq/dashboard/subscribe`) instead of the status sensor's attributes; the room result on the action sensor carries the sensors' report times instead of counters that grew every cycle; the moisture-source detector values are held while no source is active; the overnight forecast (50 mL), today's moisture balance (10 mL) and the forecast temperature change (0.2 °C) publish in meaningful steps. Calculations, decisions, the dashboard and the events are unchanged (golden master and dashboard pixel-identical); the room result keeps every reading, only the age counters became report times (see Automations). After a reload of the integration an open dashboard keeps receiving data.
- Card design follows your theme (GitHub #17) and custom styles stay (community): the card kept its own dark design in dashboards with custom themes, and design changes made with UIX or card-mod were overwritten a few seconds later on the next refresh. The card element now stays the same on every refresh and only FreshAirIQ's own content is replaced, so card-mod and UIX styles survive. New option **Colours & theme** in the card editor (YAML `theme: auto`): the card uses background, border, radius, shadow, blur, text and accent colours of the active Home Assistant theme – glass themes included – and adjusts coloured hints to the theme's text colour so they stay readable on light themes. Every colour of the card reads a `--freshairiq-*` CSS variable (card background, border, text, tiles, accent, the colours for ventilate/wait/close …) that themes, card-mod and UIX can set. Without the option the card looks exactly as before (verified pixel by pixel).
- Ventilate/Close flapping in small, humid rooms (GitHub #15): a 9.4 m³ room with surface humidity of 77–87 % was told to close about 3 minutes after the window was opened and to air again 20 minutes later, all day. Three changes: a window opened without a FreshAirIQ duration now gets a default plan of 10 minutes (within the minimum/maximum airing time) – moisture coming in, a humidity plateau and the protection limits still end it earlier; the repeat cooldown after an airing is no longer lifted just because the surface humidity is above the mould warning, only at the critical level, after a rise of at least 3 points since closing, at critical CO₂, by a strong new moisture source or clearly drier weather; and a room that was just told to close is not told to air again within the repeat cooldown unless mould or CO₂ is critical. In a simulated day of flipping recommendations the room sent 72 messages before, now 19.
- Every saved setting works at once, wherever it is changed (owner requirement): a room added or renamed – also from a room sub-entry or an automation – gets its entities and device name immediately, a shading or moisture-source sensor is switched on as soon as the room has covers or moisture sources, diagnostics settings apply immediately, and every change refreshes the dashboard at once. The settings dialog continues from changes saved elsewhere while it is open instead of overwriting them with its older copy.
- Airing that no longer helps ends (community, heinzel): a bathroom fan stuck at 59 % with a 58 % target showed "3 minutes left" for over half an hour and never said "switch off the fan". The humidity target is now recognised as out of reach when the supply air cannot get the room there, the time left follows the real (exponential) approach instead of a frozen estimate, and a sliding check ends the airing once the humidity has not fallen noticeably for 15 minutes – for fans and windows alike. A running shower or its recovery, CO₂ or cooling towards the temperature target still keep it going; a garage fan that keeps drying is not affected.
- Ventilation goals switched "off" stay off (owner report): after editing a room again, the priorities page showed the switched-off goal as active and saving made it active again. The room's general window orientation was reset the same way. Clicking through the room editor without changes now keeps every stored setting; an automatic test checks this for the room editor and for every other settings page.
- Settings work at once, without restarting Home Assistant (owner report): the automatic cover control on opening a window only started after a restart when it was switched on, or a window/cover was added, in the room editor. The same gap affected new window contacts and sensors when the room editor was closed before its last page. Every saved change is now applied to the running integration immediately; switching the control off also takes effect at once.
- Removing a sensor works again (owner report): once a per-window reference pair, window covers, room contacts, moisture sources, residents/presence entities, cross-ventilation pairs or notification targets were set, emptying the field and saving brought the old value back. Every settings form now saves an emptied field as empty. An automatic test walks all settings pages and removes every stored value once, so the same fault cannot return unnoticed.
