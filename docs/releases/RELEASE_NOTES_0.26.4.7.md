# FreshAirIQ 0.26.4.7

Performance (support exports: status attributes of 200–620 KB): the dashboard no longer receives the hourly temperature/humidity chart series of every room with every update. They made up about 37 KB per room (14 days × 24 h × 2 series), were sent twice (status entity and the room's action entity) and were re-sent every ~10 s because the newest hourly point keeps changing. The room detail view now loads them once when it is opened (`/api/freshairiq/room-history/<entry_id>`, authenticated) and refreshes them at most every 5 minutes. For 13 rooms the data pushed to every open dashboard per update drops from about 1.1 MB to about 0.18 MB (room payloads ‑90 %). The coordinator data, diagnostics, recorder behaviour and all other attributes are unchanged; older dashboards and backends keep working (inline series are still used when present). Verified in a headless browser: one request per room detail, charts rendered, no repeat requests on live updates.

New: room sensor **Empfehlung / Recommendation** (`house_aligned_action`). The existing "Aktion" sensor keeps its room-local, physical meaning (automations rely on it). The new value also respects the whole-house decision: while the house deliberately waits (better window later, pollen/fine dust, night strategy) a room that could physically be aired shows `ventilate_later` – "Lüften möglich · noch warten" – instead of "Lüften". The dashboard room tiles, the room detail view and the recommendation list use the same wording; the `freshairiq_room_action` event carries the value as `house_aligned_action` (documented in docs/AUTOMATIONEN.md). Golden master: only the new field was added; every decision is unchanged.

New (user feedback "Luftsensor draußen", Sensor.Community): **outdoor fine-dust protection**. Under Devices & services → FreshAirIQ → Configure → Outdoor air source you can now select one or more outdoor PM2.5 sensors (the highest value counts). If outdoor fine dust is above the limit (Air quality → "Consider outdoor fine dust", default 35 µg/m³), FreshAirIQ postpones normal airing exactly like the pollen rule: "Lüften wäre sinnvoll – Feinstaub draußen spricht dagegen · Fenster vorerst geschlossen lassen". Critical CO₂ or critical mould risk keep priority (short necessary air exchange). Without a sensor nothing changes (verified: all 117 golden-master cycles identical apart from four new status fields). New status attributes `outdoor_pm25`, `outdoor_pm25_limit`, `outdoor_pm25_blocked`, `outdoor_air_veto_cause`; the house status "pollen_warning" is now labelled "Außenluft belastet (Pollen/Feinstaub)"; the dashboard tile says "Feinstaub draußen beachten" when fine dust is the cause. An air purifier suggestion now also mentions fine dust.

Fix (GitHub #15 "Ventilate/Close flapping in small rooms", support export of the same installation): in a 9.4 m³ bathroom the advice switched between "Lüften", "Schließen" and "Warten" several times per hour (137 changes in four days).
- Hysteresis: a room that recommends airing keeps that recommendation until humidity falls 2 % below the start value or the drying gradient falls 0.4 g/m³ below the required value. Before, a 0.1 g/m³ wobble of the outdoor sensor flipped "Lüften"/"Warten" within seconds.
- Close threshold scaled to room size: "additional benefit in the next 5 minutes" (default 25 ml) is an absolute amount of water that a small room can never reach, so every session closed at the minimum airing time and the room re-armed soon after. Rooms smaller than 30 m³ now use a proportional threshold (9.4 m³ → about 8 ml; never less than a quarter). The same scaling applies to the live coach, the floor/house close and the planned airing duration.
- The repeat cooldown (default 120 min) now also applies to the room's own action: a room aired recently shows "Warten" (with the reason) instead of "Lüften" a few minutes after "Schließen". The house recommendation already behaved like this, so tile, sensor, push and automation events now agree with it. Critical air quality, a strong new moisture source or clearly drier outdoor air still re-arm the room immediately.
- The "close one minute after opening" part of the report is the floor/house close fix below.
- Golden master: the bathroom of the evening scenario no longer shows "Lüften" while the house recommends another room after the bathroom was just aired (27 cycles); in two scenarios a small bathroom is aired up to the learned target instead of being closed at the minimum time. All other cycles identical.
- The moisture-source headline ("Dusche erkannt") is now also kept by the wording layer, which previously replaced it with a generic "Jetzt ist ein guter Zeitpunkt zum Lüften".

Fixes from the three support exports of 2026-10-09 and the complete Hub diagnostic (Diagnostics Hub 0.3.3.82). Apart from the GitHub #15 changes above, these fixes leave every recommendation in the golden master unchanged; they add bookkeeping/diagnostic fields only (German and English verified).
- Fix (support case "Ca. 5 min nach dem Lüften sagt FreshAirIQ, jetzt ist der beste Zeitpunkt zum Lüften – ich habe gerade erst gelüftet"): after a 45-minute whole-house airing the bathroom humidity rose again by about 4 % within minutes of closing the window. That rebound (moisture released by walls, towels and furniture after airing) matched the configured bath/shower signature, so the bathroom was recommended again and the dashboard headline said "Jetzt ist das beste Lüftungsfenster". Two changes: (1) within 20 minutes after a window of that room was closed, a newly detected moisture source must either be clearly strong (≥ 1.2 g/m³ and ≥ 40 ml) or keep rising for at least 3 more minutes – a rebound flattens out, a real shower keeps climbing and is still recognised (checked against the real shower in the same export, 9 minutes after closing); (2) a moisture-source recommendation is no longer presented as "the best ventilation window": the headline names the source ("Dusche erkannt"/"Feuchtequelle erkannt"), the label reads "KURZ LÜFTEN" and the text says it applies to this room only and the rest of the home does not need to be aired again.
- Fix (same export and another one): while windows were being opened one after another, the floor/house advice flipped between "weiterlüften" and "schließen" every 10 seconds, starting one minute after the first window was opened ("Mir wurde empfohlen zu schließen"). A single room never closes before the minimum airing time (default 3 min), but the combined floor/house evaluation did. It now waits until every participating room has reached the minimum airing time; protection limits (critical mould/CO₂, hard close) and a unanimous close of all rooms still close immediately.
- Fix (support case "Benachrichtigungen für Räume ohne Berechnung und Sensoren"): rooms kept only as building structure or as live-value display (calculation off) reported their intended state as a sensor problem and triggered "Sensoren prüfen" push notifications. Only calculated rooms can raise sensor alerts now.
- Performance (all three exports showed storage-write latency incidents): a suppressed or undeliverable notification (cooldown, quiet hours, room not selected, notify service not yet available) was traced again every 10-second cycle, each time forcing an immediate full write of the learning store and pushing real deliveries out of the 50-row trace. Repeats are now folded into the previous trace row (`repeat_count`, `last_seen_at`, 30-minute window) and no longer force a write; sent and failed deliveries are always recorded individually.
- Robustness (support export with 705 failed weather fetches in 13 hours): a weather entity that never delivers hourly temperature and humidity was asked again every minute, each time with up to 10 s waiting. Retries now back off (1, 2, 5, 10, 30, then 60 minutes) and reset after the first success; the cached forecast is still kept. The health snapshot now also records why the last fetch failed (`weather_last_failure_reason`: timeout, service_error, no_hourly_rows, rows_without_temperature_or_humidity) without any provider details.
- Diagnostics (Hub cluster FAIQ-POST-001, up to 98 % of post-close observations "invalid"): sensors that report only on change are "held"/"uncertain" at the exact 10-minute mark of the post-close observation, although the window was observed cleanly. The end of the window is now judged by the last clean sample from its second half; observations without such a sample stay invalid. Diagnostic only – recommendations and learning are unchanged.
- Diagnostics (Hub cluster FAIQ-FORECAST-001): unusable forecast comparisons now carry a machine code (`invalid_code`) and the forecast-validation summary counts them (`invalid_reason_counts`, e.g. `no_frozen_start_forecast`, `moisture_source_during_session`), so exports show why comparisons could not be scored.
- The floor/house close fix above also covers the older support case "„offen lassen“ und „schließen“ für dieselben Fenster werden miteinander vermischt".
- New tests: `tests/test_support_cases_026407.py` (rebound vs. real shower, headline, sensor alerts for structure rooms, trace folding, floor close minimum duration, weather backoff and failure reasons, post-close end sample, forecast reason codes).
