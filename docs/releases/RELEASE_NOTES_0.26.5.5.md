# FreshAirIQ 0.26.5.5

Hotfix release on top of 0.26.5.4 from a community diagnosis: a door opened inside its opening delay no longer acts before the delay is over, an airing no longer flips between "Close" and "Continue ventilating" with unchanged readings, a stored close no longer blocks proven comfort cooling, a neighbouring-room humidity sensor in another unit than % is no longer read as relative humidity, exhaust fans shared by several rooms follow one common state, staggered windows keep their own minimum time and the live coach only counts rooms really aired. Golden master changed only right after a physical close (live coach). German and English verified.

## Added
- House map (owner request 02.11.): the floor plan comes first. Wind, layers and a menu sit below the plan; the menu holds the former header (recommendation, weather, forecast figures), the floors, the guests (the integration's own guest settings, changed with −/+), support (Home Assistant's page of the integration with the diagnostics download) and editing. Walls are drawn as they are: thick where a room has no neighbour, thin where two rooms share a wall (also split along a partly shared wall). Openings have their own symbols, readable without colour: window panes (open: sashes, tilted: a wedge), a door leaf with its swing, a passage as a real gap with jambs. A connection without a drawn opening shows a marker on the shared wall and is never drawn as a door; a detected moisture source shows a drop in its room; rooms with a running airing, a close advice or a critical state breathe gently (not with "animation: false" or reduced motion).
- House map editor: tap a room, then move it with the arrow keys (Shift = ten grid steps), also in Safari; the page does not scroll and undo/redo works. Dragged rooms snap onto a neighbour's wall line with a guide line and can never be dropped onto another room; corners, door and window handles are larger for fingers; walls and opening symbols as on the house map.
- Window or door per contact: every opening form has "Kind of opening" (not stated / window / door). When all contacts of the rooms in an advice are stated alike, it says "Fenster in Bad schließen", "Tür in Flur öffnen" (English "Close the windows in …", "Open the door in …"); a contact stated as a door becomes an outside door in the house map proposal. Not stated (also every older setup): the advice stays as before – nothing is assumed.
- Exhaust fan lock period: the automatic fan control of a room has an optional lock period (start and end, e.g. 22:00–07:00, also over midnight; off without both times). Inside it FreshAirIQ switches no fan on and switches off a fan it switched on – on time, also without a new reading, before moisture source, follow-up and recommendation; a fan switched on by hand or by another automation stays as it is. A fan several rooms share is not started by another room either. Status: "Ablüfter wegen eingestellter Sperrzeit ausgeschaltet"; no switch-on requests and no repeated attempts; afterwards the normal control resumes. The maximum runtime is unchanged.
- House map (owner request 31.10.): the wind is drawn as soft, translucent wave ribbons (three layered strands per stream that fade in and out at its ends) instead of dashes. The waves are moved by one continuous clock of the card – a new value, a redraw or a change of wind speed never restarts or cuts them; the air-path dashes continue on the same clock. Still with "reduce motion" or the animation off.
- House map editor: "Tap an interior door" and "Tap a passage" place a door or an open passage on a wall between two rooms (refused on an outside wall); the air paths read them as the link of both rooms. The opening tools are available without a floor plan template. The width of a selected opening can be dragged at both ends (stays centred, inside its wall, at least 0.3 m, on the grid) or changed with −/+.
- Passive test of a new moisture formula: the candidate formula `humidity-hybrid-2026-10-31-r1` is calculated next to the active start forecast and compared with the measured result – it changes no recommendation, close decision, fan, entity or learning value and is never switched on automatically. Fair pairing: both use the same frozen start (start room and reference humidity, room volume, cross-ventilation known at the opening, frozen production curve) and are read at the same measured duration; sessions with a moisture source, a reversed reference air, failed measurement gates, a cross-ventilation change during the airing, a missing measurement or values outside the calibrated range are excluded with their reason. "Wie gut FreshAirIQ aktuell vorhersagt" shows a compact section at the end (MAE of both formulas, difference, number of comparable airings, the error without any forecast – "always 0 ml", which the formula study found almost as good –, preliminary status; German and English); "better" is only reported when the test formula also clearly beats "always 0 ml". Sessions under an opening-learning quarantine are marked, not excluded; the pair per airing and a summary are part of the diagnostics. Sessions recorded before this version stay "unavailable".
- Support diagnostics need a problem description: "Diagnosedaten an Support senden" works only with at least 10 characters in "Fehlerbeschreibung (Pflicht)"; the card says why, and the integration refuses a send without one (`support_message_required`). Reports without a description could not be assigned to a case.
- House map as dashboard style: the FreshAirIQ card offers "Hauskarte (Grundriss)" under "Darstellung" next to Classic and FreshAirIQ IQ. The card then shows the house map instead of the dashboard, its editor shows the house map options and the choice to go back.
- Automatic control of a room: optional "Other automation controls the fan" (switch, helper, binary sensor or timer, e.g. Alexa after the dryer). While it is on, FreshAirIQ sends nothing to the room's fans, gives up its ownership and never switches them off; the room shows `external`.
- Fan follow-up countdown: the fan control publishes when the follow-up ends (`follow_up_until`) and when the maximum runtime stops the fans (`runtime_limit_at`) – fixed points in time, no value changing every minute. The room view shows "Nachlauf noch X min" next to the fan status, the house map room window a fan tile with the countdown or the latest stop. The 95 % air-exchange display stays separate.

## Fixes
- Staggered windows, selective close (hotfix S1, 01.11.): when a room released its own close (two fresh readings, "Close") while another window was opened only recently, the floor or house advice "keep ventilating" no longer hides it – the advice is "Raum A schließen · Raum B offen lassen", room A shows Close, room B keeps airing; the younger window still keeps its own minimum time. Rooms with a running moisture source, a fan alone and rooms in their final measurement are not affected; when every open room released its close the floor/house decision stays as it was.
- Live coach and IQ time (hotfix S2): after the floor/house decision changed a close into "keep ventilating" (or the reverse), the live-coach state and remaining time no longer describe the earlier decision; an unknown remaining time no longer reaches a number conversion.
- Exhaust fans switched by hand (hotfix A1/A2): a room's own fan that FreshAirIQ had switched off on its own while a shared fan kept running is no longer switched off again with the shared fan after the user switched it on by hand (it is taken over again only by a new automatic start); a shared fan switched off by hand is no longer switched on again by another room that needs it – every room sharing it waits until its own demand has ended.
- House map re-audit (31.10.): another FreshAirIQ entry in the card settings or a new Home Assistant connection moves the two live subscriptions to the new target (before, the old ones stayed and the new target got none); ordinary updates never subscribe again. Layout answers that arrive out of order no longer bring back an older floor plan, and only one layout request runs at a time (a newer revision is fetched right after it). A finger held on the card keeps live updates back for as long as it is down (before, only for 8 seconds); a lost release ends after a minute without any touch event, when the page is hidden or the window loses focus. Unknown forecasts (`null`) are shown as "–" instead of 0 ml in the house map header and the room window.
- House map, page jumping up (hotfix F02, user report iPhone): every live update created the card frame (`ha-card`) anew. Home Assistant draws a new card frame a moment later; the map measured the page in that moment, the card was only a few pixels high and the browser cut the page scroll back – on iPhone (no scroll anchoring) the view jumped up with every update. The card frame and an open room window now stay the same elements, only their content is updated: the page, the focused button and an open room window (no new focus, no scroll jump) stay where they were, also in the floor plan editor (opening and closing it, selection, inputs with their caret, undo/redo, saving). A live update waits while a finger or the mouse is down on the card and follows right after.
- House map subscriptions (hotfix F01): parallel starts (Home Assistant sets the connection before and after the card is shown) gave four instead of two live subscriptions; a failed channel left the other one open; a card removed during the start kept its subscriptions; a rejected unsubscribe was an unhandled error. Now exactly two per shown card and none after it is removed, with one retry timer that ends with the card.
- Formula test section on phones (hotfix 31.10.): "Experiment: Neue Feuchteformel (passiver Test)" could be missing in "Wie gut FreshAirIQ aktuell vorhersagt" because every build of 0.26.5.5 used the same address (`?v=0.26.5.5`) – a phone could keep running an older cached module without the section. The registered card address now also names the build (`&b=` plus a fingerprint of all dashboard files, the shown version stays 0.26.5.5) and every part of the card loads with that address; a new build is therefore always fetched anew (once the page or app is reloaded). If the section cannot be loaded, it now says so with error code FAIQ-UI-SHADOW-001 instead of being left out. The section appears only in the quality view (before also in the other learning views) and also without any comparison ("Noch kein Vergleich").
- Display of the house decision (re-audit 31.10.): the house map names "Lüften möglich · noch warten" (a room the house holds back, waiting colour) and "Daueröffnung überwachen" (a long opening the house tolerates, colour of a supervised airing) instead of "Unbekannt", in German and English and in the room window. Its headline shows a pollen hold, a sensor problem and an upcoming airing with their own symbol and colour instead of a green check; an "okay" published with a warning and an unknown kind are never shown as a plain check. The Classic room tile and the room detail colour a tolerated long opening like the IQ cards (blue, open window) instead of the close colour; a real or protective close keeps its colour. Decisions and texts are unchanged.
- Interior doors and passages (hotfix F01/F05): editor and air paths use one rule – the whole stored width of the opening must lie on a wall stretch the room shares with one other room on the same floor (wall distance up to 5 cm on both sides; before, the editor accepted 25 cm and the air paths 5 cm, so a passage could appear as an outside opening). A shared corner point or a too short shared stretch is no interior wall any more; the editor fits a new door or passage onto the shared stretch (width at most its length, centred) and marks an opening that no longer fits as "counts as an outside opening".
- Formula test statistics (hotfix F02/F03): records dated in the future no longer enter the "last 30 days" (accepted up to 10 minutes ahead for clocks running slightly fast); a "better than always 0 ml" statement needs a known "always 0 ml" error for every pair of the headline – with partly unknown values the known error stays visible, but "better" is not reported.
- House map wind (hotfix F04): switching the animation off or turning on "reduce motion" in the system stops the running wind animation at once (also while the card is open); switching it on again continues the waves without a cut. Leaving the card stops it, several cards keep one animation each.
- Formula test statistics (re-check N01–N05): mixed time stamps (with and without time zone) no longer switch the comparison off; "last 30 days" counts back from now, not from the newest airing; an unknown "always 0 ml" error stays unknown instead of counting as 0 ml; only pairs of the current formula pair enter the numbers (other formula versions are listed apart and the card says how many were left out); equal reports of one airing are chosen by a fixed rule. The CSV export also shows the version at the airing start, the test formula and both errors of a valid pair; the PDF stays the airing record without formula data.
- Ventilation log (audit F02): every new entry of the long-term log keeps the version that logged it, the version at the start and the formula ids of the forecast (and the errors of a valid formula-test pair); the CSV export has two more columns at the end (FreshAirIQ version, forecast formula). Older entries stay empty – nothing is filled in afterwards. The PDF is unchanged.
- Formula test statistics (audit F03): one report per airing whatever the order – a valid pair is no longer pushed out by an earlier exclusion of the same airing; exclusions count once per airing.
- Notifications, title (notification hotfix F1): the title names the action – "FreshAirIQ · Wohnzimmer · Jetzt lüften", "· Jetzt schließen", "· Kühlen", a reminder "· Erinnerung: …". A fan FreshAirIQ switches itself stays a status without a task, a close of a fan-only airing says "Lüfter ausschalten" (never "close the window"), the house push of such an airing is "Automatische Lüftung" instead of "Jetzt schließen". German and English; room names stay as written.
- Notifications, text (notification hotfix F2): plain text in a fixed order – the recommendation, "Warum:" with at most three reasons, then details (forecast) – instead of one run-on paragraph; the house push the same. A room whose action follows the house no longer quotes the reasons of its own contradicting action. `freshairiq_notification` keeps its fields and categories; `message` has line breaks, `speech` stays one readable line.
- Explanation (notification hotfix F3): "mindestens X g/m³ nötig" names the threshold the decision really used – with an active moisture source "bei aktiver Feuchtequelle genügen 0.4 g/m³" (2.3 g/m³ was explained with 2.5), with the hysteresis of a running recommendation and the dehumidify profile their lowered values, at high humidity the smaller threshold. The moisture source shows "Modellindikator 72/100" instead of "72 % Sicherheit" (a heuristic, not a probability). Thresholds and decisions unchanged (golden master: reason texts only).
- Room push after a failed send (notification hotfix F4): a room push that no notify service accepted (service missing, error) is tried again once a minute for up to 30 minutes while the action is unchanged and still open – once accepted, never twice, also after a restart. A push held back by the options, the night window, the room selection, presence or the cooldown is not retried; the event is not repeated. "Close was said" (no "ventilate" right after it, GitHub #15) is stored only once the close message is out. The house push retry is unchanged.
- Exhaust fan after a moisture source (Simon42 #167/#170/#181): in the mode "after a moisture source" the fan no longer stops at the end of its follow-up time while the room's valid recommendation still says to keep airing after the shower; it stops once that recommendation ends. Only inside the moisture-source recovery window (at most 15 min after the source) and for a fan FreshAirIQ runs itself – the maximum runtime and another automation's lock still apply, shared fans are held the same way.
- House map on phones: the floor plan fits the card (no 520-px minimum width and no sideways scrolling), stays centred, labels keep at least 12 px; a live update keeps where you scrolled in the map and the room window.
- Notifications (GitHub #15): the house push "open" no longer follows a house push "close" within the repeat cooldown (they had separate cooldowns); it is sent once the time is over. Critical mould risk or CO₂ is never held back. Room messages already followed this rule.
- Exhaust fans, control removed (hotfix F1): switching off one room's fan control no longer switches off a shared fan another room still holds (follow-up time, off confirmation) or another automation controls; it goes off once nobody holds it. A fan removed from a room's list is switched off when FreshAirIQ had switched it on.
- Exhaust fans, deferred switch-off (hotfix F2): per device – a fan nobody needs any more goes off at once, a fan still held keeps running and follows later; each switch-off is followed to its result (no double commands, nothing lost while another automation controls a fan).
- Exhaust fan wording (hotfix F3): "Lüftungsziel erreicht – FreshAirIQ prüft kurz und schaltet die Lüftung dann aus" while the switch-off waits for its 30-second confirmation; "beendet die Lüftung" only once the command is sent; "abgeschlossen" only when every fan reports off – a fan without a state after the switch-off is never shown as finished (`fans_unknown`, new label key `stop_check`; event fields unchanged).
- Exhaust fan groups (hotfix F4): when only part of a group FreshAirIQ started came on, the start is asked once more before it is reported as incomplete; a group partly switched on by hand is still never taken over.
- Moisture coming in (audit): a window where the reference air really adds moisture is closed even inside the configured minimum airing time (room and live coach).
- Moisture source follow-up (audit F02): the follow-up counts from the end of the source (`moisture_source_last_ended_at`), not from the start of the airing – a shower that just ended after 12 minutes of airing no longer closes while the air outside is still drier. Unknown end: as before. Not when the reference air is not drier or outdoor air is blocked.
- Mixed actions (audit F03): a running moisture source no longer hides another room's close – "Schlafzimmer schließen · Bad offen lassen" instead of only "Wirksame Lüftung weiterführen"; room actions, house-aligned actions and the push follow.
- Performance gate (audit P01): `evaluate_room` had become about 55 % slower than the accepted baseline over several releases. The close decision of a running airing is evaluated only while it runs, and the fine-dust check only with a reading – identical decisions (golden master unchanged), now about +20 %.
- House map card: `custom:freshairiq-house-card` was registered only by the old loader, which Home Assistant no longer loads – the card never appeared in the card picker and a YAML card stayed empty. The card module now registers it.
- Shared exhaust fans (F01): an automation lock protects the device in every room that uses it – a room never switches a fan on or off that another room's automation (Alexa, dryer …) controls right now; its private fans keep working.
- Reference humidity (F02): every open air source is unit-checked before the choice – a sensor in another unit than % can no longer hide behind a valid one (the incoming air is then unknown, never the outdoor air).
- Opening delay (F03): a door opened for the first time during a running window airing waits for its own delay before its air counts; only openings the airing already counted re-open at once.
- Staggered windows (F04, GitHub #23): a floor or house close waits until a window opened later has had its own minimum time – one room's own close or every room saying "Close" no longer ends it early. Moisture coming in still closes at once.
- Learning fallback (F05): when the learned exchange model does worse than physics ("regressing"), the room decision, the 5-minute check, costs and the forecast all use the physical base rate – before, only the forecast did. Nothing learned is deleted.
- Live coach (F06, GitHub #24): only rooms really aired right now count – a room already closed and waiting for its final measurement no longer sets the airing time of a window opened later, and the coach closes only after the youngest window's minimum time. A house close never sets a room's action to Close before the room's own close is released (moisture coming in excepted). Golden master: only the three cycles right after a physical close change (the live coach no longer times them by the closed room).
- Opening delay: a door or window opened inside its configured delay (e.g. 120 s) does not count yet – neither before an airing nor during an airing by the exhaust fan alone. Passing through no longer ends the fan's demand, switches the airing type or the reference air, changes the advice or publishes a house message; the room's `open` value keeps showing the physical state, a running window airing keeps its immediate re-open rule.
- Close decision: a stored close is lifted only when its reason is gone. A measured humidity plateau is no longer undone by a large forecast in the next cycle (community diagnosis: 85 changes "Close" ↔ "Continue ventilating" in 15 minutes with unchanged readings). Comfort cooling lifts a stored close when it is proven: temperature goal on, clearly cooler reference air without noteworthy moisture, two confirmed readings and a measurement frame that is not stale.
- Reference humidity: a humidity sensor that states another unit than % (for example an absolute-humidity sensor in g/m³) is not read as relative humidity. The neighbouring-room pair and opening-specific pairs then give no value (never the outdoor air instead); the room says why (`reference_unit_issue`, reason text) and the diagnostics carry the finding without entity IDs. Before, 9.79 g/m³ was read as 9.79 % and made the supply air look far too dry (2.1 g/m³).
- Shared exhaust fans: the follow-up time of every room sharing a fan keeps it running (the room that switched it on no longer switches it off early); the maximum runtime protects the device also while another room still needs it, and that room does not restart it until its own demand ends; all rooms show the shared fan as FreshAirIQ's (`shared`), never as switched on by hand – no "switch off the fan" request while FreshAirIQ runs it for another room.
