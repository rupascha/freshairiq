# FreshAirIQ 0.26.5.4

Hotfix release on top of 0.26.5.3: the automatic exhaust fan no longer switches on and off in feedback and its messages say what FreshAirIQ does itself, cooling advice in the comfort profile follows each room's comfort target, and the lower limit for cooling can be set in the comfort profile, an aired room shows how much of its air has been exchanged, the dialogs cover the screen again with UIX, card-mod and glass themes, and exhaust fan rooms, CO₂ and cooling advice no longer contradict each other. Golden master: recommendations unchanged except that rooms in the comfort profile at or just above their comfort target are no longer asked to air for cooling and a room aired by its exhaust fan alone is no longer part of a floor airing. German and English verified.

## Fixes
- Second follow-up audit (F01–F04): the result of switching off a room's own fan next to a shared one was not saved when nothing else changed – it is saved now; after switching off a room's fan control, a fan that did not go off stayed hidden once the shared fan was switched off later – the switch-off now stays "failed" until every fan FreshAirIQ switched off reports off (a late "off" still resolves it); the moisture-source blueprint no longer switches off a fan after a restart or reload just because it is on – only when the moisture source reports a valid "off" and ended less than the follow-up time plus 10 minutes ago (FreshAirIQ keeps this time over a restart), and then only for the remaining follow-up time; a fan without such a source (e.g. switched on by hand), a sensor without a valid state, or – with a source filter – an ended source FreshAirIQ can no longer name after the restart is left alone; the dashboard keeps its "live connection disrupted" notice when only an old payload of another FreshAirIQ entry is left. Golden master unchanged.
- Follow-up audit of this release (N01–N05, S13, S14): when room A switched off only its own fan because room B still needed a shared one, a fan that did not react got the switch-off again every 30 seconds and its result was never recorded – the switch-off is now sent once and followed to "confirmed" or "failed" ("The exhaust fan could not be switched off automatically – please switch it off by hand"); after switching off a room's fan control the result of the switch-off already sent is recorded even while a shared fan still waits for another room (a shared fan alone waits without timeout); a room aired only by its exhaust fan was told to switch the fan off at 1,450 ppm CO₂ because the drying difference was small – CO₂ above its hold band now keeps the fan running, as for windows; the moisture-source blueprint switches a fan off that was left running when Home Assistant restarted (or automations were reloaded) during the follow-up time – it runs the full follow-up time once more; the device blueprint also syncs after reloading automations and waits for a room sensor that does not exist yet at the start. Dashboard: without the live channel the card shows the status and the rooms from the sensors with the notice "Live connection to FreshAirIQ disrupted – limited view from the sensors, retrying" instead of an empty card. Card option: the FreshAirIQ look is now `freshairiq_design: freshairiq | auto`; `theme: auto` / `theme: native` / `theme: freshairiq` work as before, a named Home Assistant theme in `theme:` (e.g. `theme: FrostedGlass`) makes the card follow that theme instead of being read as the FreshAirIQ design, and the editor keeps the theme name. Golden master unchanged.
- Consolidated audit of this release (S01–S19): a close remembered earlier won over CO₂ that needed the air again (1,100 or 1,450 ppm still said "Close") – CO₂ above its hold band now lifts it and the close is decided anew, protection limits such as the maximum duration still close; a short CO₂ sensor outage ended the 50 ppm hold band – only a valid reading below the band ends it now; a fan group with one fan "off" and one "unavailable" was confirmed as switched off – a switch-off is confirmed only when every fan reports off; when room A runs a shared and a private fan and room B still needs the shared one, A skipped its whole switch-off – now A's private fan goes off and the shared one follows once nobody needs it; switching off a room's fan control no longer switches off a fan another controlled room still needs (it follows when that room is done); a switch-off still pending before a restart is followed even when no room is controlled any more; a partly started fan group is shown as "Not every exhaust fan came on – please check the others" (state `partial`, the user has to act, pushed as a task with reminders) instead of "Automatic airing is running". New event `freshairiq_fan_automation` (additive): fires when the automatic fan control changes its state, command result or need for the user while the room action stays the same. The house event no longer takes "Window 1" → "Window 2" for the same instruction (only the running minutes are ignored). A room removed and added again under the same key starts silently instead of replaying its old action. The two device blueprints bring the device in line once when Home Assistant starts (the announce blueprint stays silent) – re-import them to get this. Golden master: no decision changes; the kitchen keeps its CO₂ hold band during the sensor outage of the sensor-trouble scenario.
- Preventive audit of this release (independent physics, decision and automation audit): a CO₂ airing closed inside its 50 ppm hold band (e.g. at 975 ppm with the warning at 1000 ppm) because the small-return and the humidity-plateau close only looked at the warning threshold – the hold band now applies to every close path; a CO₂ sensor without a value (unknown/unavailable) was taken for "CO₂ is down" and ended a CO₂ airing after a few minutes – now the airing follows the duration planned when it was opened and the room says "The CO₂ sensor reports no value; CO₂ is not assessed" (a switched-off CO₂ goal behaves as before); a humidity value that was merely held by a silent sensor counted as a humidity plateau and closed an airing that was still drying – a plateau now needs at least one new humidity report in its 15-minute window (a sensor that keeps reporting the same value still makes one); with several exhaust fans of which only some came on, the start counted as failed, FreshAirIQ let go of the group and the fan that did come on ran on unattended – the start is now "partial", FreshAirIQ keeps the fans it switched on and switches them off as usual; the fan automation shows how many of several fans are on (`fans_on`, `fans_total`, `group_incomplete`) – a fan switched on by hand is still respected, FreshAirIQ does not start the others; the switch-off sent when a room's fan control is switched off is now followed until the device reports off (confirmed) or the timeout (failed) instead of staying "sent". Unchanged by decision: inside the planned airing time a close is still allowed when the reference air is at most the close difference (0.4 g/m³) drier. Golden master: no decision changes, only the new fields.
- A closed window is no longer told to keep ventilating: after a window is closed, FreshAirIQ waits a few seconds for the final sensor readings of the airing. During that wait the room still counted as being aired, so for one more cycle the room showed "Continue ventilating" (sometimes "Close") for a window that was already closed, the whole-home decision could say "keep the floor ventilating together", and the room was not yet held by the repeat cooldown. Now a room in its final measurement counts as closed for the room advice, the whole-home and floor decision and the room display, the repeat cooldown starts at the moment the window was closed, and a window opened meanwhile does not join the finished airing. The learning and the evaluation of the airing are unchanged. Golden master: in each affected cycle the rooms and the home show what they showed one cycle later before (e.g. the kitchen "Wait" instead of "Continue ventilating" right after closing).
- CO₂ is a goal of its own from the warning threshold (owner decision on an independent review): a room with CO₂ above the warning threshold (e.g. 1220 ppm at 1000 ppm) was told "Wait – airing not effective enough" when its humidity did not call for airing; the CO₂ goal disappeared behind the humidity start threshold. Now such a room is asked to air ("Airing lowers the CO₂; a short, thorough airing is enough"), with guard rails: only with the CO₂ goal switched on; not when the reference air is clearly more humid (more than 0.4 g/m³ – then it stays "wait" with the CO₂ reason); pollen and fine-dust limits still veto it; the critical threshold stays the health override; 50 ppm hysteresis. An airing asked for CO₂ alone is a short airing: once CO₂ is 50 ppm below the warning threshold it may be closed, unless the room has meanwhile become a humidity case, a moisture source runs, cooling towards the temperature goal is still useful or protection is urgent; a fan-aired room is not asked to switch on again just because the fan still dries. The humidity hysteresis of the room is not touched by a CO₂ airing. Golden master: the kitchen of the test home (1180 ppm, warning 1000 ppm) is now asked to air with the CO₂ reason and gets a "ventilate" message; when it is aired, it is asked to close once CO₂ is down (before it stayed open until the end); the whole-home decision is otherwise unchanged. Also: enumerations of goals ("Currently achievable: Dehumidification, CO₂/air quality") are now translated to English item by item.
- Fixes from an independent code review: (1) Mould risk with an exhaust fan: in a room whose fan draws its replacement air from the neighbouring room, the simplified wall model compared the room with that warm supply air instead of the outdoor air, so the surface humidity – and the mould warning – came out far too low exactly in bathrooms with a fan (e.g. 72 % room humidity: about 72 % instead of well above 80 % at 9 °C outside). The wall model now always uses the room's default reference (outdoor air); which air the fan or a single window draws in only decides the airing. (2) A fan whose switch-off the device reports a few seconds late was shown as "switched on by hand" in that moment, which could bring back the "switch off the fan" message; FreshAirIQ's own switch-off is now "Airing goal reached – FreshAirIQ ends the airing" until the device confirms it. (3) A command to a device that reports no state (unavailable) no longer stays "pending" for ever but fails after 60 seconds like any other. (4) With several exhaust fans in one room a switch-on only counts as done when every fan reports on. (5) Switching the automatic fan control off while FreshAirIQ runs the fan records the switch-off and its result. (6) The fan command trace in the support diagnostics now also records how each command ended (confirmed or failed). (7) The air-exchange display stops at 95 % ("practically complete") instead of showing 99 or 100 %. Recommendations unchanged (golden master identical).
- Clearer why sharing diagnostics helps (owner request): the hint on the dashboard (shown to administrators while no decision has been made) and the "Diagnostics sharing" settings page now say what sharing is good for – new rooms learn faster because starting values come from the measured airings of many homes, forecasts become more accurate for everyone, faults are found even when nobody reports them – and state plainly that nothing is sent without consent. The settings page also no longer claims that sharing is on by default during the beta or that it only goes to a local staging hub: sharing is opt-in, encrypted via HTTPS and runs nightly after consent. Nothing else changes: still pseudonymised, still opt-in, the card still never writes the setting itself.
- Start model for new installations (owner request): FreshAirIQ's learning moves a room's exchange rate by at most 15 % per airing, so a room whose real rate is far from the built-in starting value (0.03/min open window, 0.015 tilted, 0.04 cross ventilation, 0.02 exhaust fan) needs many airings until its forecasts fit. The diagnostics hub (0.3.3.92) now derives starting values per way of airing and room size from the measured exchange rates of real airings in other homes and recommends a value only when it rests on at least 8 rooms from 5 installations and – computed without the installation being checked – fits that installation's rooms clearly better than the built-in value. FreshAirIQ can ship such a start model (`start_model_data.py`, taken over only with a release via `tools/apply_start_model.py`, only recommended values); it only sets the starting value of a room or way of airing that has not learned yet, and the room's own learning takes over from its first airing. This release ships no start model yet, so nothing changes. Each finished airing now reports its measured exchange rate in the session record of the support diagnostics (`learning_observation`, no entity IDs), which the hub needs for the start model; the room learning in the diagnostics shows whether a start model was used (`start_model`). Golden master: recommendations unchanged (only the new session field).
- Exhaust fan, bedroom and CO₂ advice no longer contradict each other (support diagnosis): a laundry room without a window contact but with an exhaust fan was told "Air via a connected room"; when its fan started, the bedroom flipped between "Close" and "Continue ventilating" every ten seconds and the laundry room between "Close" and "Continue ventilating" although no value changed; the room names of the final decision did not match its rooms; a living room at 1445 ppm CO₂ (warning 1200 ppm) was shown "Ventilate for cooling" and only "Ventilate" from the critical 1450 ppm; and a fan FreshAirIQ stopped at its maximum runtime produced "switch off the fan". Causes: the floor and house decisions counted a running exhaust fan like an opened window – the fan room was put into the floor airing, told to "close" with the others and the close gate waited for its measurements, so both rooms followed the floor airing instead of their own models; the floor decision replaced the room keys without the matching names; CO₂ above the warning threshold did not lead when the room could also be cooled; FreshAirIQ's own switch-off was not recognised as automatic. Now a room aired by its exhaust fan alone is judged by its own room model and is no part of a floor or house airing (rooms with an opened window are unchanged); names and keys of the decision always match; a room without a window contact but with an exhaust fan says "Switch on the exhaust fan" (the action code stays `ventilate_indirectly`, events say "Please switch on the exhaust fan"), a room without a window names its configured open connection ("aired through the open connection to Hallway") and otherwise claims no connection; with CO₂ at or above the warning threshold and cooling possible the room is asked to "Ventilate" with CO₂ as the leading reason and the cooling as an extra benefit, the critical threshold stays the health override, and the advice ends only 50 ppm below the warning threshold (no flicker around it); a CO₂ goal switched off keeps the cooling advice. A fan FreshAirIQ switched off itself is reported as "Automatic airing finished". The support diagnostics also contain each room's comfort target and its source, the action before and after the house alignment, the close readiness and the fan hint. Golden master: only the fan-only room of the exhaust scenario leaves the floor airing (the bedroom keeps its own recommendation), plus the matching room names and a new CO₂ hysteresis value in room memory.
- Dialogs cover the screen again with UIX, card-mod and glass themes (support case): after switching from card_mod to UIX all FreshAirIQ dialogs (details, room overview, room details, support, guests) appeared inside the dashboard card, and the lowest room of the room overview was cut off and could not be reached. The dialogs are fixed overlays inside the card; since 0.26.5.3 the card element stays the same, so styles from UIX, card-mod or a glass theme now stay on it – and a backdrop-filter, transform or contain on the card or on any parent (layout cards, UIX) makes that element the frame of every fixed overlay. The dialogs now open in the browser's top layer, which always covers the screen whatever the card and its parents look like; they keep their styles, theme colours, scroll positions, Back navigation, live updates, touch scrolling and closing. Theme support, UIX and card-mod styles are unchanged; browsers without this feature (before iOS 17 / Chrome 114) keep the previous behaviour.
- See how far an airing has exchanged the room air (community question): while a room is aired, its tile in the room overview and the opened room show how much of the room air has been replaced so far and how long it takes, under the current conditions, until 95 % is exchanged ("Air exchange 62 % · about 9 min to 95 %"; "practically complete" from 95 %; "estimate" while the room has not learned its airing yet). It uses the room's learned exchange rate for the way it is aired (window, tilt, exhaust fan) together with the wind and cross-ventilation factors of the model; a second opened window or a change of wind shortens the time at once. 100 % is never reached physically, so the count goes to 95 % (about three air changes). Display only: no goal and no priority, recommendations and messages are unchanged; the values reach the dashboard over the live channel and are not written to the room entities (no extra recorder rows).
- Automatic exhaust fan without on/off feedback and with honest messages (support diagnosis, community): with the fan control "following the recommendation" a garage fan went on and off within one second, and FreshAirIQ asked to "switch off the fan" although it switches the fan itself. Switching the fan changed the very evaluation that switched it: a running fan is an airing with its own close checks, the house decision ended the planned airing, the fan went off at once, and the next evaluation without the fan said "air" again – the fan went on again right away (a room without a window contact even escaped the repeat cooldown). Now a switch-off asked for by the recommendation must last 30 seconds, and after a run FreshAirIQ ended itself the fan starts again only after the anti-flap time (15 min) – unless a moisture source is active or CO₂ is critical. Moisture coming in, the maximum runtime, switching the control off and a manual switch-off still act at once; a fan switched on or off by hand is respected (one flicker of the recommendation no longer lifts a manual switch-off). A command counts as done only once Home Assistant reports the new state; a command that is not carried out is shown as failed and the user is asked to switch by hand. While FreshAirIQ switches the fan itself, the dashboard, events and pushes say what the automation does ("Automatic airing is running", "Airing goal reached – FreshAirIQ ends the airing", "Moisture source ended – automatic follow-up time running") instead of "switch off the fan"; such pushes are status messages and follow "Send status messages". A fan with the neighbouring-room reference and the automatic control is judged on the air it will draw in already before it starts, the room's reasons say so, and the fan texts no longer speak of outdoor air in that case. Events keep their action codes and gain `fan_automation`; the support diagnostics list each fan command with its reason and result (no entity IDs). Recommendations are unchanged (golden master).
- Cooling advice in the comfort profile follows the room's comfort target (GitHub #19, community): a living room at 24.3 °C with a manual comfort target of 25.5 °C was told "Ventilate for cooling", and a room at exactly its thermostat target of 24.0 °C showed "Ventilate for cooling" while the whole home said "target reached, keep closed". A new cooling airing only looked at the house-wide start temperature (24 °C) and ignored the room's own target. In the comfort profile a room with a temperature goal (manual, thermostat or fallback target) is now only asked to air for cooling once it is 0.5 °C above its comfort target – the same margin that already keeps a running cooling airing open – and never below the house-wide start temperature, so no room is asked to cool earlier than before. The single window assessments use the same target. Unchanged: the summer cooling profile, rooms without a target, a switched-off temperature goal (no cooling advice), humidity, CO₂ and protection limits, and how running airings are continued and ended.
- New setting for the comfort profile (GitHub #19): the house-wide room temperature from which airing for cooling may start was used by the comfort profile too, but could only be set in the summer cooling profile. Devices & services → FreshAirIQ → Configure → Ventilation & recommendations → Operating profile now also opens a page for **Comfort** with this one field. It is renamed in both profiles to **Cool from room temperature (lower limit)** (German: „Kühlen frühestens ab Raumtemperatur“, formerly „Sommerkühlung ab Raumtemperatur“); its help explains that rooms with a comfort target are only cooled 0.5 °C above that target. Default and stored value stay the same (24 °C).
