# FreshAirIQ 0.26.6.1

Hotfix release on top of 0.26.6.0: dialogs stay on top during live updates (#20), cross-ventilation pairs accept room names (#25), an optional Home Assistant Mold Indicator per room for the surface humidity (#21), and a larger, readable floor plan editor (community feedback).

## Fixes
- Dialogs on top (#20): a live update patched the open details, rooms or support window against freshly rendered HTML and removed the `popover` attribute set at runtime – the window fell out of the browser's top layer and was cut to the card (UIX, card-mod, glass themes, transformed parents). The live patch now keeps that attribute and lifts the overlays again afterwards; nothing else in rendering, CSS or scrolling changed.
- Cross-ventilation pairs with room names (#25): "Schlafzimmer+Wohnzimmer,Esszimmer+Küche" was stored as typed and silently ignored, because the recommendation, the cross-ventilation detection and the house strategy only understood internal room keys. One shared resolution (exact key first, then the unique room name – case and umlauts as in the room-key convention) now serves the options flow and every reader; only keys are stored ("schlafzimmer+wohnzimmer,esszimmer+kuche"). Malformed, unknown, ambiguous, same-room and repeated pairs are rejected in the options flow with the reason (DE/EN). Pairs stored with names by an older version are migrated once and idempotently at start; valid key pairs stay exactly as they are, what cannot be resolved is kept in the option and logged once per Home Assistant run. Floor rules, contact debouncing and learning are unchanged.

## Added
- Mold Indicator per room (#21): a room can name Home Assistant's "Mold indicator" helper (only entities of that integration are offered; area import suggests it and no longer offers it as the room humidity). A valid reading (finite, 0–100 %, unit %) replaces the internal surface humidity estimate inside the room evaluation – before mould risk, urgency, the ventilation advice and the close decision. Without a helper, or with an unavailable or invalid reading, the internal estimate is used exactly as before (formula, global surface factor and all limits unchanged; room air, absolute humidity, ventilation physics and learning untouched). The value stays an estimate. Rooms with a helper publish `surface_rh_source` (`ha_mold_indicator` / `internal_estimate`), the helper entity and the reason of a fallback; diagnostics show the source but never the helper's entity id. Each room uses its own source.

## House map editor (community feedback)
- The floor plan editor opens in its own workspace over the whole screen (top layer, independent of the dashboard card's width or a transformed parent); the house map stays underneath and comes back on save or cancel.
- A steady drawing: its size comes from the layout alone, never from the properties, a message or a hint. On a wide editor and on short landscape screens (e.g. 844 × 390) the properties stand beside the drawing at a fixed width; on tall narrow screens (tablet portrait, phone) they take a fixed share below it and scroll on their own. Choosing a room, wall, window, door or corner, a tool or a panel never shrinks or moves the drawing (on a 768 × 1024 tablet it shrank from 511 to 465 px; at 844 × 390 drawing, properties and toolbar overlapped – both fixed).
- A calm toolbar: floor, Quick | Precision (clearly told apart, each with a short explanation), "+ Room", "Window", "Door", "Passage" and "More" (grid, snap to 90°, fit rooms together, propose again, add missing rooms, export, import, help). Undo/redo and zoom/fit float at the bottom corners of the drawing, save and discard stay under the properties.
- Short hints over the drawing say what to do with the chosen object or tool ("Tap an outside wall to place a window.") and otherwise the next sensible step (rooms missing, no window yet, openings without a contact, save). The empty properties show the steps of the workflow with ticks: floor, rooms, position and shape, windows/doors/passages, contacts, check and save – no forced wizard.
- "+ Room" opens a small panel: which room, its shape as pictures (rectangle, L, U, own shape = rectangle in Precision mode to drag the corners), add.
- Properties by object: a room shows area, "Add window", "Rotate 90°", "Edit corners" and its floor, with the corner coordinates and the connections without a shared wall in sections that open on demand; a wall its length, the corner that stays, the lock and its angles; a window or door its kind, width, hinge/opening (doors) and the contact assignment first, position on the wall and mechanism in sections. On a phone "Drawing only" gives the drawing the whole height; the selection stays and "Edit" brings its properties back.
- Messages float over the drawing, can be closed and name a way out (e.g. a corner that cannot be removed, an invalid plan when saving, a file that cannot be imported, a failed save keeps the draft).
- Readability: all editor colours come from the Home Assistant theme through editor tokens; if a theme's text, muted text or accent does not reach WCAG AA contrast against the editor background, a checked light or dark palette is used. Text sizes at least 14 px, controls 44 px, the active tool and the chosen object (thicker outline, marked properties) stand out. The house map and other cards are not restyled.
- The width handles of a selected opening no longer cover the middle of a small door or window, so it can be moved there (it was only widened before when drawn large).
- All functions, the stored plan (no new schema), the geometry, the house map and every FreshAirIQ calculation are unchanged; Escape now also ends an active tool.
