# FreshAirIQ 0.26.6.0

House map release (owner request 02.11.): the floor plan fills the whole card and the air moves through the house the way it can.

## Changed
- House map: the plan fills the card – no frame, tiles or text around it. The menu button lies on the plan at the bottom right; the menu holds the layers (action, temperature, humidity, CO₂, air paths, quality), the details (recommendation, weather, forecast figures), floors, guests, the notes about the drawing (proposal, estimated room sizes, unconfirmed north, "wind streams are schematic", the air-path model) and support/editing. The wind tile is gone: the animated streams show where the wind comes from (the wind figures stay in the details). Warnings that need a tap (another floor needs action, live connection disrupted, auto focus paused) lie on the plan at the top left.
- House map wind: air coming in through an open window moves through the house only where rooms are connected – through the drawn door or passage between two rooms, else through the middle of the wall the plan connects them by; never straight through a wall. Where two rooms have no such place the stream ends. At the other end it streams out of the open window again and fades outside the house; a room with a second open window lets the air out there when no air path is known. The air-path layer follows the same passages.

## Fixes (hotfix 0.26.6.0)
- Exhaust fan lock period: when the switch-off at the start of the lock period failed (call refused, or the fan never reported "off"), the room lost its ownership at once – the fan ran on through the night, nothing tried again and the status read "stopping". The failure now keeps the run FreshAirIQ's and the failed state; the switch-off is tried again after 10 minutes, at most three times in all (no endless retries); the status says "could not be switched off automatically – please switch it off by hand" and the trace records each result. A fan switched off and on again by hand, or taken over by another automation, stays the user's; an unknown fan state is no proven switch-off.
- House map editor: the plan's connections are shown in the editor too – as a logical link (dashed, its kind as a symbol), apart from the drawn doors and openings; a connection whose rooms already have an opening on their shared wall is drawn once, as that opening. A door or open passage without a wall the two rooms share is marked ("no shared wall").
- House map editor: arrow keys, Delete and Backspace change the plan only while the drawing has the keyboard; a button, a field or a select keeps its own keys and never moves or deletes a room.
- House map guests: the guest settings are found like on the FreshAirIQ dashboard card – by the entity's FreshAirIQ key and the card's config entry (a renamed entity still works, a second FreshAirIQ entry is never changed; with several entries and none known the count is only shown) – and stay within 0–20.
- House map air: a door, window or passage counts as a way between two rooms only when its whole span lies on the wall both rooms share – computed from the current plan, so a stale target after a room was moved is never used; a door or open passage connection only where the rooms share at least 0.3 m of wall (a corner, a gap or another floor is none; stairs, shafts and ducts are no way through a wall). The air is never drawn through a wall without such a place. The layout check reports both cases as warnings (`W_OPENING_NOT_SHARED`, `W_CONNECTION_NO_WALL`); saving stays possible.

## House map and touch editor (0.26.6.0, second build)
- Walls: the house map and the editor draw the very same walls (one function): exterior walls thick, a wall two rooms share once and thin – also where it is shared only partly. The rooms no longer have an outline and a shadow of their own (that made shared walls look like exterior walls); one shadow lies under the whole house. A door or open passage is a real gap in the wall on both sides, exactly as wide and where it is; moved or removed, the wall closes again.
- Doors, windows and passages: drawn light with a dark rim, readable on every room colour; interior and exterior doors have the same symbol; the state keeps its own colour band. Doors can have a hinge side (left/right, seen from inside the room) and open into the room or to the other side – set by tapping the door in the editor, drawn with leaf and swing arc; whether a door is open still comes from its contact. Plans without these settings stay as they were.
- Floors: every other floor (and "All floors") is a button at the bottom left of the plan; the floor shown is not offered. A floor where an action is needed (air now, close, critical) pulses gently – not for waiting, a running airing or normal values; with reduced motion it keeps a static mark. The notice "action on another floor" is gone; auto focus and the manual floor choice work as before.
- Menu: a real window over the plan (layers, details and figures, guests, notes about the drawing, support, editing) that stays open during live updates; the floors are no longer in it.
- Support: opens its own window over the house map with the support functions of the FreshAirIQ card (diagnostics with required description, export, feedback, cooldowns, error codes) – the same code, only in the house map's look; it no longer jumps to the integration page.
- Editor on phones and tablets: tap a room, a wall, a door or a window to choose it; a finger moving over something not chosen moves the view, two fingers zoom, only the chosen room moves with the finger. Every wall has a wide invisible touch area. The panel sits at the bottom on phones and beside the plan on large screens.
- Wall lengths: tap a wall – its length is shown on the plan; enter a new length in metres, choose which corner stays, apply. The wall at the other corner moves along in parallel (a rectangle stays a rectangle, slanted walls work too), a wall a neighbouring room shares moves along with it; a wall shared only partly, an overlap, an invalid shape or a door that no longer fits is refused with a message. A wall length can be locked (corner drags, the size handle and later edits keep it). Undo/redo and saving include it. The house map geometry stays a drawing – Home Assistant room sizes are not changed.
- Connections: a door or open passage in the plan's connections can be turned into a drawn door or passage on the shared wall (the connection is then replaced, never kept twice).

## House map UI hotfix (0.26.6.0)
- Floors: "All floors" is gone – each card shows one floor; for several floors at once the card is added several times. Each card can have its own floor (`floor: upper_floor` – floor id, level or name; also in the card settings) and keeps its own choice, so several cards never switch each other; a stored "all floors" choice of an older build falls back to the card's floor. Floor buttons and the menu are smaller and sit in a bar at the bottom edge of the card, below the plan – they never cover rooms, walls, windows or labels; the plan's own bottom margin stays a free zone for the wind; on narrow phones and with many floors the buttons wrap.
- Support window: "Export diagnostics" and "Send diagnostics to support" stand side by side, equally wide and high, centred text (at most three lines); the window scrolls and its close button stays reachable on small screens. The functions are unchanged (required description, export, sending, cooldowns).
- Editor: an interior door or passage can no longer be dragged, widened or set (wall field) off the wall both rooms share – the move is refused with a message instead of silently turning it into an outside door. Doors and passages placed with the interior tools carry `interior: true`; such an opening off a shared wall blocks saving (`E_OPENING_INTERIOR`, in the browser and in Home Assistant). Older plans without the mark behave as before.
- Editor: schematic plans show plan units instead of metres and can get a real scale from one measured wall ("Set scale": every room and opening width is scaled alike, the plan is metric from then on; undo restores it). Metric plans are unchanged.
- Editor on phones and tablets: walls, doors, windows and passages have 44 px touch areas and corner handles 44 px; where areas overlap the nearest object is chosen (a door before the wall it lies on, the wall of the room tapped into). Handles are shown only for a chosen room or corner, so a chosen wall keeps its neighbours tappable.

## House map look (0.26.6.0, sixth build)
- Rooms show their state as their surface: a calm, cool tone when all is well, muted amber while waiting, brighter green/cyan/orange where something is to do (air now, keep airing, close) and coral with a faint hatch when critical; a soft inner rim glows gently on rooms that need an action (not with "animation: false" or reduced motion – the room breathing now also stops with reduced motion, which it did not before).
- The white label boxes are gone: the room name stands on the room surface in a colour chosen for contrast (≥ 4:1) with a soft halo; the state is a small dark capsule (symbol and short word) and large rooms add temperature and humidity below. Small rooms show name and capsule, tiny rooms only the state symbol; texts never leave the room (shortened instead, never below 12 px).
- Door swings are a quiet hint (hairline, finely dashed, half transparent); the door leaf carries the door.
- Windows on an interior wall (e.g. a guest WC window from a proposal that found no free outside wall, or after a room was moved) are found in the editor: "N windows sit on an interior wall – move all to outside walls" and per window "Move to the outside wall" put them centred on the room's nearest outside wall (one undo step, saved as usual). Corner and width handles in the editor are drawn small, their touch area stays 44 px.

## House map labels and weather (0.26.6.0, seventh build)
- Room labels always stay inside the room's real outline – also in L- and U-shaped rooms, which are no longer measured by their bounding box: the label goes where the room is wide enough (measured line by line), and the richest variant that fits is shown (name, state and readings; then without readings, a smaller name, the short state word, only the state symbol, a shortened name). Text widths are measured, not estimated. A room too small for any text shows its state as a dot sized to the room (at least 18 px); name and values are one tap away.
- Local weather top right: outdoor temperature and humidity from FreshAirIQ, or the card's own sensors (`weather_sensors: sensor.a, sensor.b` – also in the card settings), each with its unit; unknown values are left out. It sits in a slim bar above the plan together with notes that need a tap, so nothing covers the plan. The live dashboard data carry `outdoor_temperature` and `outdoor_humidity` for it (additive).

## House map atmosphere and mould limits (0.26.6.0, eighth build)
- Labels: a room keeps its temperature and humidity before the state word – the readings may stand in two rows (°C, %) and the state may shrink to its symbol; the name may get smaller. Labels keep door swings and open sashes free. A long name in a small room is shortened to its last word ("Gäste-WC" → "WC") before an ellipsis; a very small room shows its short name alone, a tiny one a state dot; the full name is always the label's title. Cause of the missing readings in bedroom and bathroom: readings existed only in variants with the full or short state word, which did not fit next to a long state such as "air possible · wait".
- No background grid: the plan lies under a sky that follows the real weather – a calm gradient, a soft depth and a soft shadow under the house instead of the hard offset. Day, twilight and night come from `sun.sun` (elevation) or Home Assistant's "clear night" condition, never assumed. At night the sky turns deep night blue, the rooms lie in a cool, dim light (walls, symbols and labels stay fully readable above it) and stars appear – many on a clear night, a few through broken clouds, none when overcast; at twilight a soft violet veil and the glow on the sun's side.
- Weather around the house, only from Home Assistant data (FreshAirIQ weather summary and `sun.sun`): sunlight with soft rays from the sun's side (only with a known azimuth), twilight glow, stars on a clear night, drifting cloud shadows, fog bands, rain and snow slanted with the wind (about 5° per m/s), a faint flash with thunder. Nothing is drawn without data; the weather is kept off the house (rooms, labels and controls stay clear).
- Room climate at rest (from the room's readings; nothing for stale data): warm rooms glow softly warm, cool rooms cool (20–22 °C neutral); humid rooms carry a soft mist (from 60 % RH, livelier and marked "↑" while humidity rises ≥ 3 %/h), dry rooms a fine dry texture (below ~38 %); the model's mould risk level shows as speckles along the walls (four steps); an active moisture source lets steam rise; a running exhaust fan turns slowly. Rooms at rest breathe very gently, airing/close/critical rooms as before.
- Airing: fresh air reaches into the room at an open outside window as a soft cone (shorter when tilted, none while the opening delay is running), fine particles travel with the wind streams into the room, through doors and passages and out of the other window; after closing the air fades out over a few seconds. A modelled picture, not a flow simulation.
- Look: one calm family of room surfaces (no more yellow rooms – waiting is a quiet blue-slate), slimmer softer walls, quieter door swings, seven wind streams with shorter turns along the walls (no crowding).
- All animations stop with "reduce motion" and the card's animation option and pause while the card is hidden.
- Mould risk limits: "Advanced model parameters" → "Mould & CO₂" now sets all four limits of the estimated surface humidity – slightly elevated (default 60 %), elevated (70 %), high (80 %), very high (90 %). They must rise strictly; existing settings are kept; an older "high" limit at or below 70 % keeps its value and the two new limits are placed just below it. The limits are used for the room's mould risk level everywhere.

## House map comes alive (0.26.6.0, ninth build)
- Labels (regression of the eighth build): the swing of every door and the sash area of every window – also closed ones – were kept free of labels; in small rooms only the poorest label variants were left, so temperature and humidity disappeared (e.g. study, children's room, bathroom) and names were cut ("Arbeitszim…", "Bad…"). Now only door swings and open sashes are kept free, and only as long as a variant with readings still fits – readings come first, then the swing. Compound names break into two rows ("Arbeits-" / "zimmer"), the state symbol may stand in front of the name, the readings below; names are no longer cut while a room has space.
- Wind: the air around the house is now a modelled wind field drawn on a canvas layer over the plan – fine, softly glowing threads travel with it and leave fading trails, a few brighter heads give depth, and the earlier wind streams became broad, nearly clear air masses whose bend changes slowly. The field follows the measured wind direction and speed: the threads begin to bend before an outside wall, slow down in front of the windward wall and split towards its ends, glide along the facade and speed up past the corners; behind the house the air is calmer. Behind the corners where the flow separates, soft eddies roll up from time to time, grow, drift downstream and dissolve – larger and livelier with stronger wind. Gusts are events (rise, peak, decay) sized by the measured gust when Home Assistant reports one, otherwise a small decorative variation. Weak, moderate and strong wind differ in density, speed and eddies. No wind data: no wind is drawn. A modelled picture – not a measurement, not a flow simulation.
- Airing: through an open outside window part of that air flows into the house without a break – narrow at the window, fanning out in the room, narrow again at each door or passage, out through the other window (cross ventilation through several rooms); a tilted window lets in less and narrower air. In a room told to close, the air moves calmer and warmer. After closing, the air fades out over a few seconds. Labels, walls and symbols stay clear of it.
- Rooms: a measured drying (humidity falling ≥ 3 %/h) shows as "↓" next to the reading and a clearing mist; rising humidity as before ("↑", livelier mist).
- Top left: the whole house's removable moisture (theoretical, all floors, every room once, only valid readings) and FreshAirIQ's recommended airing time – while airing the time left; with little forecast confidence rounded to 5 minutes. New values glide there instead of jumping. A tap explains the values (removable vs. reachable within the airing time with the learned air exchange, confidence, per room). The live channel carries `theoretical_potential_total_ml` additively.
- Animation: one loop per card at about 30 frames per second; the air layer pauses with the card and stands still (a calm picture of the streamlines) with "reduce motion" or the animation off. No new dependency (canvas 2D, no WebGL).

## House map: the same values as the card, wind that follows the weather (0.26.6.0, tenth build)
- Removable moisture top left is now exactly the FreshAirIQ card's value. Cause of the difference: the ninth build showed the theoretical value of a complete air exchange (`theoretical_potential_total_ml`, or a sum of the rooms) and rounded the time itself, while the card shows FreshAirIQ's "removable now" – `potential_total_ml`, what the learned air exchange of every calculated room is expected to remove within the recommended airing time (sensor "Removable moisture"), and while airing the amount removed so far. Now the house map calls the card's own function (`removableNow` in freshairiq-card.js): same data, same rule, same rounding; the extra live value is gone again.
- The time top left is that recommended airing time ("≈ 1947 ml in 12 min") – the amount belongs to exactly this time; while airing "so far ≈ 322 ml · 5 min left" as on the card. While the house decision says "wait", the amount is shown as possible and no time. The explanation on tap says so.
- Wind character from the real weather (Home Assistant data only: mean wind and measured gust, weather condition, rain/thunder, sun.sun): sunny – fewer, warm-white, long soft threads, slower gliding, gentle and rare eddies, softer air layers; neutral – calm and even; rain – a little denser and cooler; lively – more layers and eddies; storm – bundled, fast, crisp threads, tighter curves and large rolling eddies, fuller air layers. Gusts without a measured value stay a small decorative variation that suits the mood; a measured gust always sets the size. Labels stay clear in every weather; the weather never changes a room's state or the recommendation.
- Phone: the info bar above the plan breaks into two rows instead of letting moisture and weather overlap.

## Hotfix after the independent review (0.26.6.0, eleventh build)
- Moisture signs (F01): the shared helper took the absolute live balance and cut negative potential to 0 – a balance of −322 ml (moisture added) appeared as "so far ≈ 322 ml", an airing that would add moisture as "0 ml". Now signs are kept: removed shows "≈ N ml", added "+N ml added" / "+N ml would be added" – on the house map, in the IQ view and as before in the classic balance; no success claimed for an increase.
- Window inflow (F02): the air entered only where one of the fixed decorative streaks happened to cross an open window – the same window one metre further along the wall got none. Now every open, confirmed outside opening the wind presses on (published facade pressure ≥ 0.3, the airflow model's inlet threshold) gets its own inflow, wherever it sits; the canvas air inside follows it.
- No invented cross flow (F03): without a known air path, any other open window of the room was used as the outlet – also on the same facade. Now the air leaves the house only along a directed path the airflow model published; otherwise it stays in the room.
- Unknown wind speed (F04): a wind with a direction but a missing speed or an unsupported speed unit was marked available. Now it is `available: false` with the reason `no_speed` (different from a measured calm); the airflow model stays topological and the map shows "speed unknown" and no wind animation.
- Session state (F05): the IQ view and the house map counted a session only until the window closed, while the backend's live balance and remaining time include its final measurement. Both now count it until the final measurement, like the backend and the classic panel.
- Time over target (F06): a remaining time of −3 min appeared as "3 min left" (house map) or "3 min" (IQ view). Now: "3 min over target" / "+3 min".
- Wait and night (F07): the house map used its own narrow wait check. It now takes the IQ view's own decision (`_iqFacts` in freshairiq-card.js: wait, pollen wait, night strategy, delayed release, running session), so it shows a time exactly where the IQ view does.

## House map editor, sun position and README (0.26.6.0, twelfth build)
- Wall lengths, one wall at a time: a new length changes only that wall – the chosen corner stays, the other corner moves along the wall and the next wall takes up the change. The opposite wall keeps its length and a room may stop being a rectangle. A wall shared with a neighbouring room no longer drags the neighbour along; growing into it is refused with an explanation ("would overlap the neighbouring room – fit the rooms together instead"). Before, both corners moved (the opposite wall changed too).
- Locks: besides a wall's length (`locked_walls`) the angle at a corner can be locked (`locked_corners`, new optional layout field, normalised in Home Assistant like `locked_walls`). Later edits carry locked walls and angles along; when an edit cannot keep every lock nothing changes and the message names the wall or corner and a way out. The editor never saves a changed locked value.
- 90° corners: the wall panel shows the angle at both corners (inner corners of L and U rooms as 270°) and offers "Corner … to 90°" for that one corner – the turning wall keeps its length, nothing else is squared. "Snap to 90°" in the toolbar makes a dragged corner near a right angle (±6°) snap to it exactly; the grid still applies.
- Fit rooms together: rooms measured one by one and pushed close (up to about 35 cm apart, also with slightly different lengths such as 3.92 m and 3.98 m) are fitted into one house. Every room only moves as a whole – no scaling, no bending, locked dimensions win; corners line up along shared walls where nothing is locked. A preview shows the result (dashed rooms, shared walls highlighted, notes on different lengths, locked corners and overlaps) and nothing is saved before "Apply"; undo/redo work. Shared walls then count as interior walls (drawn once), doors on them link both rooms (also for the air paths in Home Assistant), windows stay outside.
- Door swings in the editor only: the house map draws the door leaf with its state but no swing arc any more, and a closed door no longer reserves label space for an arc nobody sees. The editor keeps the arc for setting hinge and direction.
- Sun position: the sun on the house map follows `sun.sun` during the day – redrawn when azimuth or elevation moved by 2° (about every eight minutes), not on every Home Assistant state change; before, it moved only when the day phase changed. Its side follows the plan's north; without a confirmed north the map shows even daylight from above, no sun disc and no rays.
- Floor names: the editor shows the same readable floor names as the map's floor buttons (ground floor, upper floor, basement, attic …); internal ids are unchanged.
- Documentation: README (EN/DE) presents the house map with pictures and a feature overview; house map guides describe the new editor tools; outdated "All floors" picture replaced. Pictures are renders of this release's card code with demo data.

## Hotfix after the independent review of the twelfth build (0.26.6.0, thirteenth build)
- Window sensor in another room (F01): where a window is drawn, which sensor it has and which room it airs are now independent. A guest WC window whose contact is set up for the hall can be drawn in the WC: the map, the opening details and the air paths take the sensor's live state (and its configured facade) from the room that has it in its setup – the physical room first. The editor offers every contact of the setup for an opening, the room's own first, the others marked with the room they air. The hall's decision is unchanged (the plan never feeds the decisions).
- Shared contacts are no longer doors by themselves (F02): a contact used by two rooms is drawn as a door between them only when it is known to be a door – stated as a door or as a door to a neighbouring room in the setup, or a door sensor in Home Assistant (device class door/garage_door). A shared window – and a shared contact of unknown kind, like an unknown contact of a single room – is drawn once as a window and is no connection: in the room that states it as a window, else the room that lists fewer contacts (a hall borrows its neighbours' windows), else the smaller room. A proposed shared door now sits in the middle of the stretch both rooms share and is no longer moved along with windows on the same wall.
- Rooms drawn clockwise (F03): the outside of a wall is taken from the polygon's real orientation (JS and Python alike). Fitting rooms together now finds the matching walls of a clockwise room, and a clockwise room's windows face the right way for the wind (before: 180° off). Corner order, opening walls and locks are unchanged.
- Locked angles and corners (F04): a locked angle is followed by its corner, not only by its index – adding or removing a corner next to it, or removing the locked corner itself, is refused with its own message ("changes a locked angle"); a corner added or removed elsewhere keeps the lock on the right corner.
